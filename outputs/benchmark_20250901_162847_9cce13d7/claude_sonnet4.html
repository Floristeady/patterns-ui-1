Traditional Temples

Morning: Senso-ji Temple

Art & Museums

Cultural Heritage

Morning: Tokyo National Museum

Discover Japan's largest collection of cultural artifacts, including samurai swords, ancient pottery, and Buddhist art.

Afternoon: Ueno Park

Stroll through cultural district, visit multiple museums, and experience traditional Japanese garden aesthetics.

Food Culture

Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market

Experience authentic sushi breakfast, learn about Japanese ingredients, and observe traditional food preparation methods.

Traditional Arts

Hands-on Learning

Morning: Tea Ceremony

Learn the ancient art of Japanese tea ceremony, understand the philosophy of wa, kei, sei, jaku (harmony, respect, purity, tranquility).

Afternoon: Calligraphy Workshop

Practice shodo (Japanese calligraphy) with traditional brushes and ink, create your own artistic piece to take home.

Modern Culture

Contemporary Japan

Morning: Harajuku & Omotesando

Explore youth culture, street fashion, and contemporary art galleries. Witness the blend of traditional and modern aesthetics.

Evening: Kabuki Performance

Experience traditional Japanese theater at Kabuki-za, learn about this UNESCO-recognized cultural art form.
