6-Day Tokyo Culture Trip Planner

Day 1: Arrival and Shibuya Exploration

Morning: Arrive at Haneda Airport and check into the hotel.

Afternoon: Visit Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko Statue.

Evening: Dinner at a local Izakaya and explore Shibuya nightlife.

Day 2: Historical Insights in Asakusa

Morning: Visit Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise Shopping Street.

Afternoon: Explore Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center.

Evening: Traditional Japanese tea ceremony experience.

Day 3: Art and Innovation in Roppongi

Morning: Visit Mori Art Museum and Roppongi Hills.

Afternoon: Explore teamLab Borderless Digital Art Museum.

Evening: Dinner with a view at Tokyo City View restaurant.

Day 4: Traditional Tokyo in Ueno

Morning: Visit Ueno Park and Tokyo National Museum.

Afternoon: Explore Ameya-Yokocho Market.

Evening: Enjoy a traditional Kabuki performance at National Theatre.

Day 5: Modern Tokyo in Shinjuku

Morning: Visit the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observatory.

Afternoon: Explore Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.

Evening: Experience the vibrant nightlife in Kabukicho.

Day 6: Cultural Finale and Departure

Morning: Participate in a traditional Japanese cooking class.

Afternoon: Visit Meiji Shrine and Harajuku district.

Evening: Farewell dinner and departure from Narita Airport.
