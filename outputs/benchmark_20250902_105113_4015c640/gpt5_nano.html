Tokyo Cultural Journey

A 6-day, culture-focused timeline through Tokyo’s temples, markets, tea rooms, and modern districts.

6 DAYS • TOKYO

Day 1 — Arrival & Asakusa

1

Nov 3 • Welcome to Tokyo. Settle into your Shinjuku hotel and ease into the rhythm of the city.

  • Morning: Flight into Tokyo, transfer to hotel in Shinjuku.
  • Afternoon: Senso-ji Temple, Nakamise Shopping Street, and traditional storefronts.
  • Evening: Street-food stroll and ramen along Asakusa's historic lanes.

Day 2 — Meiji Shrine & Harajuku

2

Nov 4 • A morning of traditional Tokyo culture and modern street style.

  • Morning: Meiji Shrine and a calm walk through Yoyogi Park.
  • Afternoon: Takeshita Street fashion and Omotesando architecture stroll.
  • Evening: Izakaya crawl in Shibuya to taste regional yakitori and sake.

Day 3 — Tea Ceremony & Kabuki

3

Nov 5 • Deepen cultural appreciation through ritual and performance.

  • Morning: Traditional Japanese tea ceremony with ceremonial sweets.
  • Afternoon: Tea room demonstration and a stroll through a historic district.
  • Evening: Kabuki performance at Kabuki-za in Ginza.

Day 4 — Edo Tokyo & Akihabara

4

Nov 6 • A day of history, tech, and bright shop windows.

  • Morn: Edo-Tokyo Museum to explore life in the old city.
  • Aft: Akihabara district tour with retro game shops and electronics.
  • Evening: Optional river cruise along Sumida for skyline views.

Day 5 — Toyosu Market & Sushi Class

5

Nov 7 • Fresh fish markets and hands-on cooking.

  • Morning: Toyosu Market guided tour and sushi breakfast.
  • Afternoon: Sushi making workshop with a local chef.
  • Evening: Sunset views at Odaiba and Rainbow Bridge.

Day 6 — Modern Tokyo & Departure

6

Nov 8 • A final morning of green spaces and last-minute culture reminders.

  • Morning: Stroll through Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.
  • Afternoon: Quick Ginza shopping for crafts and souvenirs; depart to airport.
  • Farewell: Reflect on culture-filled moments and connections made.
