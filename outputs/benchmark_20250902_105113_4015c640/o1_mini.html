6-Day Tokyo Cultural Trip

Day 1: Arrival and Orientation

Date: July 1, 2024

Arrive in Tokyo, check into your hotel, and attend a welcome orientation session to go over the trip itinerary and cultural etiquette.

Day 2: Traditional Tea Ceremony and Kimono Experience

Date: July 2, 2024

Experience a traditional Japanese tea ceremony and learn to wear a kimono with the guidance of a local expert.

Day 3: Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise Street

Date: July 3, 2024

Explore the historic Senso-ji Temple and stroll along Nakamise Street to enjoy traditional snacks and shop for souvenirs.

Day 4: Edo-Tokyo Museum and Asakusa Culture Tour

Date: July 4, 2024

Visit the Edo-Tokyo Museum to learn about Tokyo’s history and participate in a guided culture tour of the Asakusa district.

Day 5: Cooking Class and Ikebukuro Exploration

Date: July 5, 2024

Join a hands-on Japanese cooking class and explore the vibrant Ikebukuro area, including shopping and entertainment options.

Day 6: Sumida River Cruise and Farewell Dinner

Date: July 6, 2024

Enjoy a scenic cruise along the Sumida River and attend a farewell dinner featuring traditional Japanese cuisine and performances.
