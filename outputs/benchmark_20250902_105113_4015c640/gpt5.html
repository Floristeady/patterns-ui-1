6-Day Tokyo Culture Trip Planner

A horizontal timeline you can swipe through

Learn Tokyo through temples, museums, crafts, and food

Tip: Swipe left/right on the cards below (or use the small dots) to move through each day of the plan.

Trip timeline

  1. Day 1 · Sat, Apr 12 · Asakusa, Taitō

    Asakusa & Old Tokyo

    Start at Senso-ji, stroll Nakamise-dori for traditional snacks, and learn about Edo-era life along the Sumida.

    • 09:00 · Senso-ji temple and Kaminarimon gate 🛕
    • 11:00 · Street food on Nakamise (ningyō-yaki, senbei) 🍘
    • 13:30 · Sumida Park walk with Tokyo Skytree views 🌳
    • 15:00 · Kimono rental and photo walk (optional) 👘
    Temples Street Food History

    Transit: Ginza Line to Asakusa. Many sites are walkable.

  2. Day 2 · Sun, Apr 13 · Ueno

    Ueno Museums & Park

    Dive into Japanese art and history at Tokyo National Museum and stroll lotus ponds of Shinobazu.

    • 10:00 · Tokyo National Museum main exhibits 🏛️
    • 13:00 · Bento picnic in Ueno Park (weather permitting) 🧺
    • 15:00 · Ameya-Yokocho market walk for snacks 🛍️
    Art Parks Markets

    Transit: JR to Ueno Station or Ginza Line to Ueno.

  3. Day 3 · Mon, Apr 14 · Harajuku & Omotesandō

    Meiji Shrine, Harajuku & Calligraphy

    Experience Shintō tranquility, youth culture, and try a shodō (calligraphy) lesson.

    • 09:30 · Meiji Jingu forest walk and shrine visit 🌲
    • 12:00 · Takeshita Street crepes and boutique browsing 🧁
    • 15:00 · Shodō calligraphy workshop (90 min) ✒️
    Nature Workshop Street Culture

    Transit: JR Yamanote to Harajuku or Chiyoda/Fukutoshin to Meiji-jingumae.

  4. Day 4 · Tue, Apr 15 · Fukagawa & Ryōgoku

    Edo Heritage: Fukagawa & Sumō District

    Step into Edo-period townscapes and explore Ryōgoku’s sumō culture.

    • 10:00 · Fukagawa Edo Museum (life-size Edo street) 🏮
    • 12:30 · Monzen-nakachō lunch: tempura or soba 🍤
    • 14:30 · Ryōgoku sumō stables area walk (exteriors) 🥋
    Edo History Neighborhoods Food

    Transit: Tozai Line to Monzen-nakachō, JR Sobu Line to Ryōgoku.

  5. Day 5 · Wed, Apr 16 · Tsukiji & Ginza

    Markets, Cooking Class & Kabuki

    Taste Tokyo at Tsukiji Outer Market, learn sushi basics, then enjoy an evening at Kabuki-za.

    • 08:30 · Tsukiji Outer Market tasting walk 🍣
    • 11:00 · Hands-on cooking class (sushi or tempura) 👨‍🍳
    • 18:00 · Kabuki-za evening program in Ginza 🎭
    Food Culture Workshop Theater

    Transit: Hibiya Line to Tsukiji, walk to Ginza for Kabuki-za.

  6. Day 6 · Thu, Apr 17 · Yanaka & Nezu

    Yanaka Old Town & Nezu Shrine

    Wander nostalgic lanes, visit artisan shops, and close with vermilion torii tunnels at Nezu.

    • 10:00 · Yanaka Ginza shopping street and cafes ☕
    • 12:30 · Handmade ceramics or incense workshop 🏺
    • 15:00 · Nezu Shrine torii path and azalea garden ⛩️
    Crafts Shrines Local Life

    Transit: JR to Nippori (Yanaka), Chiyoda Line to Nezu.

Accessibility: Most sites have step-free routes. Kabuki-za offers English subtitle guides. Check museum closures on Mondays.
