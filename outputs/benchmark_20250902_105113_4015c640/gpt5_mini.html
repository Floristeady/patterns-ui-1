6-Day Tokyo: Cultural Immersion

A day-by-day plan to learn Tokyo's history, arts, and everyday culture

Jun 3 — Jun 8 • Tokyo, Japan

Day 1 — Arrival & Intro

June 3
Arrival
  • 15:00
    Arrive at Narita/Haneda → Transfer to hotel in Asakusa. Check-in and short rest.
  • 17:30
    Evening stroll to Senso-ji, try freshly made ningyo-yaki and street snacks.

Accessibility note: cobblestone streets in Asakusa can be uneven; accessible routes are available via main streets.

Day 2 — Museums & Shrines

June 4
Culture
  • 09:30
    Tokyo National Museum — guided highlights tour to learn samurai and Buddhist art.
  • 12:30
    Lunch at a local soba restaurant in Yanaka Ginza — observe neighborhood life.
  • 15:00
    Visit Nezu Shrine and quiet shrine rituals; time for reflection.

Tip: Many museums offer English labels; Audio guides often available.

Day 3 — Traditional Arts

June 5
Hands-on
  • 10:00
    Traditional tea ceremony (chadō) with explanation of etiquette and history.
  • 13:00
    Kimono dressing workshop — try on a kimono and learn about fabrics and symbolism.
  • 16:30
    Short talk at a local craft shop about lacquerware and pottery techniques.

Accessibility: Inform hosts of mobility needs; seated ceremony options exist.

Day 4 — Modern & Pop Culture

June 6
Contemporary
  • 10:00
    Akihabara — visit a retro game shop and learn about otaku culture's history.
  • 13:00
    Harajuku walk: Takeshita Street, small boutiques, and crepe tasting.
  • 17:30
    Evening at an observation cafe to discuss modern art and design in Tokyo.

Note: Popular streets can be crowded; keep valuables secure and follow local guidelines.

Day 5 — Food & Culinary Culture

June 7
Culinary
  • 08:30
    Tsukiji Outer Market tour — sample sashimi and learn about seasonality.
  • 13:00
    Cooking lesson: make onigiri and learn the language of flavors (umami).
  • 19:00
    Izakaya crawl with a local guide — try small plates and sake tasting.

Dietary note: vegetarian and allergy-friendly options available with advance notice.

Day 6 — Reflection & Departure

June 8
Farewell
  • 09:00
    Visit Meiji Shrine for a quiet ceremony and reflection on the trip.
  • 12:00
    Farewell lunch with group discussion: what we learned and next steps.
  • 15:30
    Transfer to airport for departure; assistance provided if needed.

Final checklist: travel documents, souvenirs packed, airport transfer confirmed.
