Tokyo Cultural Experience - 6 Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival

Arrive at Narita Airport and check into your hotel in central Tokyo. Enjoy an evening meal at a local izakaya to get a first taste of Japanese hospitality.

Day 2: Museums & Temples

Visit the Tokyo National Museum to explore traditional art and history, followed by an afternoon tour of historic temples in Asakusa.

Day 3: Tea Ceremony

Participate in a traditional tea ceremony and later join a cultural workshop to learn calligraphy and ikebana flower arranging.

Day 4: Pop Culture & Tradition

Explore the electronic town of Akihabara to witness Tokyo's pop culture, then contrast it with calm visits to local historic neighborhoods.

Day 5: Shibuya Exploration

Wander through the bustling Shibuya district. Experience modern fashion before visiting one of Tokyo’s traditional markets for street food and crafts.

Day 6: Meiji Shrine & Departure

Visit the serene Meiji Shrine for a spiritual start and enjoy a final walking tour through traditional neighborhoods before your departure.
