Top 5 Beginner-Friendly Hiking Trails

Near Seattle, Washington

  1. 1

    Rattlesnake Ledge Trail

    Easy 2-3 hours

    A popular 4-mile round trip hike with stunning views of Rattlesnake Lake and the surrounding Cascade foothills. The well-maintained trail features a steady incline through second-growth forest, making it perfect for beginners looking for a rewarding vista without excessive difficulty.

  2. 2

    Twin Falls Trail

    Easy 2.5 hours

    This scenic 3-mile trail follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River through old-growth forest to a spectacular 150-foot waterfall. The path includes several wooden bridges and viewpoints, offering multiple photo opportunities and a gentle introduction to Pacific Northwest hiking.

  3. 3

    Discovery Park Loop Trail

    Easy 1.5-2 hours

    Located within Seattle city limits, this 2.8-mile loop offers diverse ecosystems including meadows, forests, and beach access. Perfect for beginners seeking nature without leaving the city, featuring views of Puget Sound, Olympic Mountains, and historic lighthouse.

  4. 4

    Franklin Falls Trail

    Easy 2 hours

    A family-friendly 2-mile round trip hike leading to a stunning 70-foot waterfall. The wide, well-groomed trail follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River through moss-covered forest, making it accessible year-round and especially beautiful during spring snowmelt.

  5. 5

    Little Si Trail

    Easy-Moderate 3-4 hours

    The younger sibling to Mount Si offers a 4.7-mile round trip with moderate elevation gain. This trail provides panoramic views of the Snoqualmie Valley and surrounding peaks without the crowds, featuring rocky outcrops perfect for lunch breaks and photography.
