Shinjuku

Vibrant hub where neon towers meet tranquil gardens. Ideal for the first day: easy transit, variety of activities, and kid-friendly options.

Recommended places:

  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observation deck (free panoramic views of Tokyo)
  • Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (seasonal blossoms, wide paths for strollers)
  • Omoide Yokocho (historic alleys with casual eateries)
  • Samurai Museum (interactive exhibits suited for families)

Why we recommend them: Mix of outdoor spaces, culture, and easy strolls with frequent transit access from Shinjuku Station.

Restaurants to try in Shinjuku:

  • Torikizoku Shinjuku (yakitori, affordable and kid-friendly)
  • Afuri Shinjuku (yuzu ramen with lighter broth for kids)
  • Nabezo Shinjuku 3-chome (shabu-shabu hotpot, great family dining)

Shibuya

Electric crossroads with vibrant shopping, cafés, and green spaces along Meguro River. A top pick for families who enjoy energy and easy transit.

Recommended places:

  • Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko Statue (classic photo spots)
  • Shibuya Sky (observation deck with city views)
  • Meiji Yuuen Park nearby and a stretch of Center Gai for kids’ snacks

Why we recommend them: Compact, walkable area with plenty of family-friendly options and great access to trains.

Restaurants to try in Shibuya:

  • Genki Sushi Shibuya (conveyor-belt sushi)
  • Uobei Shibuya (affordable, fast service)
  • Ichiran Shibuya (ramen with kid-friendly seating areas)

Harajuku

A playful, fashion-forward pocket of Tokyo. Great for families who enjoy unique fashion, street food, and parks nearby.

Recommended places:

  • Takeshita Street (colorful shops and quirky treats)
  • Meiji Jingu (calming forested grounds near the city)
  • Yoyogi Park (wide lawns, easy for kids to run around)
  • Omotesando (tree-lined boulevard with architecture and cafés)

Why we recommend them: Mix of energy, nature, and accessible transit for a relaxed day.

Restaurants to try in Harajuku:

  • Afuri Harajuku (yuzu ramen with lighter broth)
  • Cafe Kaila Harajuku (pancakes and breakfast-style plates)
  • Kyushu Jangara Harajuku (tonkotsu ramen, family-friendly)

Asakusa

Old Tokyo charm with Senso-ji Temple and traditional shopping along Nakamise Street. A wonderful area for family photos and gentle strolls by the Sumida River.

Recommended places:

  • Senso-ji Temple (historic temple grounds and red lanterns)
  • Nakamise Shopping Street (traditional snacks and souvenirs)
  • Sumida River stroll and nearby parks

Why we recommend them: Rich history, easy walking routes, and plenty of kid-friendly snack options.

Restaurants to try in Asakusa:

  • Tempura Daikoku Asakusa (crisp tempura in a relaxed setting)
  • Kagetsudo (melon pan and sweets, iconic in Asakusa)
  • Sushi Zanmai Asakusa (affordable sushi near the temple)

Ginza

Sleek, walkable district known for department stores, theaters, and fine dining. Good for an upscale day with kid-friendly cafés nearby.

Recommended places:

  • Kabukiza Theatre (see a traditional performance or browse the area)
  • Ginza Six and Mitsukoshi department stores (shopping and cafés)
  • Hibiya Park nearby for a little outdoor time

Why we recommend them: Safe, clean streets with options for all budgets and tastes.

Restaurants to try in Ginza:

  • Sushi Kyubey Ginza (classic edomae style sushi)
  • Gyukatsu Motomura Ginza (breaded beef cutlet, family-friendly)
  • Ippudo Ginza (ramen with kid-friendly flavors)

Akihabara

The electric town of Tokyo—perfect for curious kids and teens who love gadgets, anime, and arcade games.

Recommended places:

  • Akihabara Electric Town streets (video game shops and character goods)
  • Mandarake Complex and other hobby shops (collectibles and comics)
  • Radio Kaikan and arcade centers for family-friendly play

Why we recommend them: Interactive shopping and playful experiences that can be fun for all ages.

Restaurants to try in Akihabara:

  • Gundam Cafe Akihabara (themed dining for fans)
  • CoCo Ichibanya Akihabara (customizable curry bowls)
  • Popeye’s Curry or local ramen spots nearby for quick meals

Family Travel Tips

Balance busy days with gentler mornings, plan transit between neighborhoods, and schedule mid-afternoon breaks at comfortable cafés or parks.

Practical notes:

  • Use a Suica or Pasmo card for quick transit on JR and метро lines
  • Carry bottled water and light snacks for day trips
  • Look for family-friendly restrooms and stroller-friendly routes

Restaurant tips: Favor casual, kid-friendly spots with English menus or picture guides.

Seasonal Highlights

Tokyo shines differently through the year. Spring brings blossoms in Ueno and Shinjuku; autumn paints gardens in gold and red; winter glows with city illuminations.

Nice seasonal ideas:

  • Cherry blossoms at Ueno Park and along the Sumida River (late March–early April)
  • Autumn foliage at Rikugien Garden and Koishikawa Korakuen
  • Winter illuminations in Roppongi Hills and Marunouchi (November–January)

Family angle: Plan some days around daylight hours and indoor activities during peak weather. Always check opening times in advance.
