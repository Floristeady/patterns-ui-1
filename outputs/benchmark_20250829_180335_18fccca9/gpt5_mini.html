Shibuya

A lively mix of iconic crossings, family‑friendly parks, and easy shopping for all ages.

Good for: energetic days, shopping, easy transport ›
Why visit

Shibuya captures Tokyo's energy—great for families who want iconic photos (Shibuya Crossing), easy walking, and multi‑floor department stores with kids' floors.

Recommended places
  • Shibuya Crossing & Hachiko statue
  • Shibuya Scramble Square observation deck
  • Center Gai and Magnet by Shibuya 109 (family-friendly shops)
  • Yoyogi Park (short walk) for open playtime
Restaurants
  • Uobei Shibuya (conveyor sushi) — fun for kids
  • Afuri (yuzu ramen) — milder broths available
  • Hacienda del Cielo (rooftop Mexican) — relaxed patio

Asakusa

Historic Tokyo centered on Senso‑ji shrine with pedestrian streets perfect for families.

Good for: culture, easy strolling, souvenirs ›
Why visit

Asakusa offers a slower pace, classic temples, and Nakamise Street with snacks and simple crafts kids enjoy.

Recommended places
  • Senso‑ji Temple and Kaminarimon gate
  • Nakamise shopping street (traditional snacks)
  • Sumida Park and river cruise options
  • Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center (observation deck)
Restaurants
  • Daikokuya Tempura — classic tempura (family portions)
  • Asakusa Menchi — local fried meat patties, good snack
  • Kaminarimon Sando — quick casual and kid‑friendly

Shinjuku

Vibrant transport hub with parks, department stores, and family entertainment options.

Good for: central transit, nightlife-free family spots ›
Why visit

Shinjuku blends green spaces (Shinjuku Gyoen) with department stores and easy train connections—ideal as a base for families.

Recommended places
  • Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden — wide lawns and seasonal flowers
  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observation decks (free)
  • Takashimaya / Isetan food floors for quick family meals
Restaurants
  • Omoide Yokocho for quick yakitori (note: crowds; some stalls family‑friendly)
  • Fuunji — famous tsukemen (check wait times with kids)
  • Beard Papa (cream puffs) — sweet treat for children

Ueno

Great museum district and Ueno Park—perfect for educational and relaxed family days.

Good for: museums, zoo, relaxed walks ›
Why visit

Ueno offers museums and open spaces; the zoo and the National Museum of Nature and Science are kid favorites.

Recommended places
  • Ueno Zoo (good for younger children)
  • Tokyo National Museum & National Museum of Nature and Science
  • Shinobazu Pond (boat rentals available)
Restaurants
  • Izuu (traditional sushi in nearby Yanaka)
  • Innsyoutei (tempura in Ueno Park) — peaceful setting
  • Street food stalls on Ameyoko Market — quick bites

Odaiba

Waterfront entertainment island with malls, teamLab Borderless (or similar), and kid activities.

Good for: hands‑on attractions, views, evening lights ›
Why visit

Odaiba is designed for leisure—spacious malls, interactive museums, and waterfront promenades where kids can run.

Recommended places
  • Miraikan (National Museum of Emerging Science) or teamLab-style digital art museums
  • Palette Town / DiverCity (shopping + giant Gundam statue)
  • Odaiba Seaside Park and Rainbow Bridge views
Restaurants
  • Aqua City food court — many kid options
  • Gundam Cafe (novelty, fun for kids)
  • Yakiniku / family restaurants in Decks Tokyo Beach

Akihabara

Tech and pop‑culture district with toy stores, arcades, and family‑friendly gadget shops.

Good for: arcades, shops, anime fans ›
Why visit

Akihabara delights kids who love games, toys, and quirky cafes; arcades and character shops are highlights.

Recommended places
  • Retro and new game arcades (SEGA, Taito Station)
  • Figure and toy shops along Chuo Dori
  • Mandarake (multi‑floor pop culture store)
Restaurants
  • Curry Shop CoCo Ichibanya — familiar and kid‑friendly
  • Maid cafes (some are family‑friendly daytime options) — check reviews
  • Poke bowls and themed cafes in nearby streets

Trip Overview

Suggested pacing and a simple one‑week plan to fit the six neighborhoods.

Good for: planning your week ›

Day suggestions: Shinjuku + Shibuya (arrival), Asakusa + Ueno (museum & park), Akihabara (arcades), Odaiba (digital art & sea views), flexible day for shopping or favorite repeat.

Keep travel times short and alternate busy & relaxed days to keep kids happy.

Family Tips

Practical advice for traveling in Tokyo with children.

Good for: comfort & logistics ›
  • Bring a lightweight stroller for museums and parks; many stations have elevators but plan routes.
  • Buy an IC card (Suica/Pasmo) for easy transit and vending machines.
  • Choose midday rest windows—shops and department store food floors are great for quiet breaks.

Transport & Passes

How to get around efficiently with a family.

Good for: saving time & hassle ›

Get Suica or Pasmo cards for adults and children. Consider a prepaid data SIM or pocket Wi‑Fi. Taxis are convenient for short legs with luggage but can be costly.
