Top 5 Beginner-Friendly Hiking Trails

Near Seattle, Washington

  1. 1

    Rattlesnake Ledge Trail

    2-3 hours Easy

    A popular 4-mile round trip hike featuring stunning views of Rattlesnake Lake and the surrounding Cascade foothills. The well-maintained trail includes switchbacks that make the elevation gain manageable for beginners. Perfect for families and offers spectacular photo opportunities at the ledge viewpoint.

  2. 2

    Twin Falls Trail

    1.5-2 hours Easy

    This scenic 2.6-mile trail follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River to a spectacular 150-foot waterfall. The path features wooden bridges and boardwalks through old-growth forest. Ideal for beginners seeking a rewarding waterfall experience without excessive difficulty.

  3. 3

    Discovery Park Loop Trail

    1-1.5 hours Easy

    Seattle's largest city park offers a gentle 2.8-mile loop through diverse ecosystems including meadows, forests, and beach areas. Features panoramic views of Puget Sound and the Olympic Mountains. Perfect for urban hikers looking for nature without leaving the city limits.

  4. 4

    Little Si Trail

    3-4 hours Moderate

    A 4.7-mile round trip hike offering breathtaking views of Mount Si and the Snoqualmie Valley. The trail winds through second-growth forest with moderate switchbacks. Less crowded than its bigger sibling Mount Si, making it perfect for those ready to advance from easy trails.

  5. 5

    Franklin Falls Trail

    1-2 hours Easy

    A family-friendly 2-mile round trip trail leading to a stunning 70-foot waterfall. The wide, well-groomed path follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River through old-growth forest. Spectacular in winter when the falls can freeze, creating dramatic ice formations perfect for photography.

Always check trail conditions and weather before hiking. Carry water, snacks, and appropriate gear.
