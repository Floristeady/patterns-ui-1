🗾 Tokyo Cultural Journey

6 Days of Discovery

Day 1
⛩️

Traditional Tokyo

Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, Tokyo's oldest temple. Experience traditional shopping at Nakamise Street and participate in a tea ceremony.

Morning: Senso-ji Temple & Asakusa Shrine

Afternoon: Traditional Tea Ceremony

Evening: Yakitori dinner in Memory Lane

Day 2
🎌

Imperial Heritage

Explore the Imperial Palace East Gardens, visit the Edo-Tokyo Museum, and learn about samurai history at the Samurai Museum.

Morning: Imperial Palace & Gardens

Afternoon: Edo-Tokyo Museum

Evening: Kabuki performance in Ginza

Day 3
🍱

Culinary Culture

Morning visit to Tsukiji Outer Market, sushi-making class, and explore depachika (department store food floors) for regional specialties.

Morning: Tsukiji Market & Breakfast

Afternoon: Sushi Making Workshop

Evening: Izakaya hopping in Shinjuku

Day 4
🎨

Arts & Crafts

Visit teamLab Borderless digital art museum, try traditional origami and calligraphy workshops, explore Harajuku's modern art scene.

Morning: teamLab Borderless

Afternoon: Calligraphy Workshop

Evening: Roppongi Art Triangle

Day 5
🌸

Nature & Zen

Morning meditation at Meiji Shrine, stroll through Shinjuku Gyoen gardens, experience an onsen (hot spring bath) for relaxation.

Morning: Meiji Shrine & Forest

Afternoon: Shinjuku Gyoen Gardens

Evening: Onsen Experience

Day 6
🏮

Modern Traditions

Experience a sumo practice session, shop for traditional crafts in Yanaka, farewell dinner with kaiseki cuisine.

Morning: Sumo Stable Visit

Afternoon: Yanaka Old Town

Evening: Kaiseki Farewell Dinner
