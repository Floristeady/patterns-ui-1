Top 5 Beginner-Friendly Hiking Trails Near Seattle

Perfect trails for those starting their hiking journey

1

Rattlesnake Ledge

Easy 2-3 hours

A popular 4-mile round trip trail offering stunning views of Rattlesnake Lake and the surrounding Cascade foothills. The well-maintained path features a steady incline perfect for beginners looking to build stamina. The panoramic viewpoint at the top makes every step worth it.

2

Twin Falls Trail

Easy 2.5 hours

This scenic 3-mile trail follows the South Fork Snoqualmie River through old-growth forest. Multiple viewpoints showcase the spectacular 135-foot waterfall. The wide, well-graded path and wooden bridges make it accessible for families and novice hikers year-round.

3

Franklin Falls

Easy 2 hours

A gentle 2-mile round trip hike leading to a stunning 70-foot waterfall. The trail meanders alongside the South Fork Snoqualmie River through moss-covered forest. Perfect for beginners and families, with minimal elevation gain and breathtaking photo opportunities at the falls.

4

Little Si

Moderate 3-4 hours

A 4.7-mile loop trail offering a less crowded alternative to Mount Si. Features beautiful forest scenery and rocky outcroppings with views of the Snoqualmie Valley. The moderate elevation gain provides a good workout while remaining manageable for beginners ready for a challenge.

5

Wallace Falls

Moderate 3.5 hours

A rewarding 5.6-mile round trip to spectacular waterfalls dropping 265 feet. The trail offers multiple viewpoints along the way, allowing hikers to choose their turnaround point. Dense forest canopy provides shade, making it ideal for warmer days while building hiking endurance.

Remember to check trail conditions and bring proper gear before heading out
