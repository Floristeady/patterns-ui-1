¥ Cost Analysis

Ginza and Roppongi are the most expensive areas, while Asakusa offers the best value for money. Shibuya and Shinjuku fall in the premium range due to their central location and amenities.

🛡 Safety Overview

All neighborhoods maintain high safety standards. Ginza leads with a 9.2 rating, followed by Asakusa. Even Roppongi, despite nightlife activity, maintains good safety levels.

🍜 Dining & Shopping

Shinjuku leads in restaurant density, while Shibuya and Ginza offer the best shopping experiences. Asakusa provides traditional dining but fewer modern retail options.
